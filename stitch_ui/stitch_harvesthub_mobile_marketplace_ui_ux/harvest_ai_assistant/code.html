What's the best way to store fresh farm tomatoes so they don't lose flavor?

10:14 AM
psychiatry
verified Harvest Produce Specialist 10:14 AM

Great question, Ubaid! Fresh local tomatoes should NEVER be refrigerated if they are still ripening. Chilling under 12°C permanently shuts down enzymatic volatile production, which destroys their sweet fragrance!

tips_and_updates Optimal Storage Ritual:
  • check_circle Stem-side down at room temperature (20–22°C) away from direct sun to lock moisture around the stem scar.
  • check_circle Only refrigerate if fully softened ripe and you won’t consume them within 2 days.
  • check_circle Flavor restoration tip: Let cold tomatoes rest on the kitchen counter for 30 minutes before slicing to revive natural sugars.
